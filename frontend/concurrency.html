1. Mutual Exclusion

If a process is executing in its critical section, no other processes can be executing in their critical sections. BOLT implements this using Java's ReentrantLock.

2. Progress

If no process is executing in its critical section and some processes wish to enter, only those not in their remainder sections can participate in deciding who will enter next.

3. Bounded Waiting

There must exist a bound on the number of times other processes are allowed to enter their critical sections after a process has made a request. Fair locking prevents starvation.

4. Optimistic Versioning

In addition to thread mutexes, BOLT employs @Version optimistic locking to ensure that conflicting database transactions from other JVM nodes abort safely.
