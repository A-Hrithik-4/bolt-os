1. Mutual Exclusion

At least one resource must be held in a non-shareable mode. In BOLT, each bank account can only be modified by one thread at a time.

2. Hold and Wait

A thread must be holding at least one resource and waiting to acquire additional resources held by other threads.

3. No Preemption

Resources cannot be preempted; a resource can be released only voluntarily by the thread holding it after it has completed its task.

4. Circular Wait (Prevented)

A closed chain of threads exists such that each thread holds at least one resource needed by the next. BOLT breaks this via global lock ordering!
