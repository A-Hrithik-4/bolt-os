Strategy Comparison

SAFE (ReentrantLock) vs UNSAFE (Raw)

SAFE MODE (Active Lock) SYNCHRONIZED

• Mutual Exclusion: Per-account ReentrantLock(fair=true) ensures serial access to balance variable.
• Atomicity: Deposits & withdrawals validate latest verified state.
• Transfers: Lexicographical lock ordering prevents deadlocks.
• Consistency: Guaranteed zero lost updates.

UNSAFE MODE (No Lock) UNSYNCHRONIZED

• Race Conditions: Multiple threads read stale balance before write commits.
• Lost Updates: One thread overwrites the update of another without notice.
• Discrepancy: Ledger transaction sum does not match final account balance!
